# AI Exploration Reflection: First-Come, First-Served (FCFS) Scheduling

## How I Approached Learning the Topic

At the beginning I was relying more on AI, but as I went through I found myself going to YouTube videos, since it is a method I prefer. 

## Prompts

1. **"give an example of how or when my computer uses this scheduling(FCFS), focus on giving examples"**
   I asked this because while I did understand the definition and a bit of the theory, I was still a bit lost as to when my computer would use this. From this I learned that there are multiple processes in the background that I did not account for that need these schedulers like FCFS. 

2. **"explain to me again what is i/o and how it relates to blocks and rejoins"**
   Here I was trying to link back a previous explanation to the content that is being taught from the course(i/o), in hopes to assimilate and build some connections with things I understand or I am practicing. This helped me make some connections I had not figured out by myself.

3. **"what is a cpu burst?(this was a reply to a code block I did not undesrtand)"**
   I asked this because there was a gap in the explanation that was given of a prior prompt and my knowledge, to remedy that I started asking subquestions, breaking answers into subanswers to atomic levels, so I could better understand motivations and use cases. 

## How I Engaged With AI

Mostly I did one prompt with more context as to what was happening, and started to dissect the response by highlighting text from it and asking questions. I prefer that because I can have an overview(which I did not understand in the beginning) and subgroups for each of the things I did not understand. By the end I try to reread the "master" prompt and see if I finally understand it. 

## What Worked Well

Having a master prompt which had a lot of information helped me find my weak and strong points regarding the topic, which helped me dive into the topics I was struggling the most since the beginning.

## What I Would Do Differently

In hindsight, I probably could have asked chatgpt to make me some quizzes to assess my understanding. This repetition may be very helpful and may also expose you to new examples and situations you had never previously thought of.
